# Event

An Event is something that happened and should remain findable later.

Long Events may preserve chronology or session notes, but should open with a short `## At a Glance` summary when they exceed roughly 2,000 words.

Use Event notes for meetings, conversations, rollouts, incidents, milestones, decision events, achievements, external changes, or historical records when the occurrence itself matters for future retrieval.

Use a normal Note, Project, ADR, or Source instead when the durable value is mostly synthesis, active work, a formal decision record, or external provenance rather than the fact that something happened.

Source model: [Portent Types](https://portent.md/types).
