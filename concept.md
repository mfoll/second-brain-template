# Concept

A Concept is a reusable idea, distinction, model, method, or term that may be useful across projects.

## Optional Maturity

Concept notes may use `maturity` when the maturity level improves retrieval or review. Suggested values are `Evergreen`, `Growing`, and `Seed`.

Do not add maturity metadata to every Concept by default.
