# Procedure

A Procedure is a durable how-to or runbook for performing a workflow.

A Procedure is not itself the recurring work. Operations, Projects, Skills, or humans may follow a Procedure. A Procedure should describe the steps, guardrails, inputs, outputs, and verification well enough that another human or agent can perform the workflow.

`Procedure` is a local Second Brain extension rather than a core Portent PORT action type. Use [[operation|Operation]] for recurring one-sitting work that should track cadence and operating state.
