# Maintain Vault

Maintaining a vault means keeping notes retrievable, project summaries useful, procedures current, and unprocessed captures from becoming invisible.

This Responsibility is the root of the vault's own area in the `belongs_to` hierarchy: the root Project [[vault]] belongs to it, as do the operating notes that describe how the vault works ([[adr/0011-root-belongs-to-at-responsibilities-and-home-exploration-clusters-in-topics|ADR 0011]]). A deployment with an owning Responsibility for its infrastructure work may re-parent it there.
